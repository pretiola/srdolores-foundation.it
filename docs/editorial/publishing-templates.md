Store consent records, names not approved for publication and original receipts privately, outside this repository.

## Image and video caption

## First 7–14 days after asset receipt

After verification, prepare a flagship field update, homepage priority/imagery refresh, Fr. Emmanuel interview/story, first case study and approved heritage update. Reconcile related spending evidence. Sequence publication by readiness; asset arrival does not itself authorize publication. Build a 60–90 day editorial calendar from the remaining approved material.
