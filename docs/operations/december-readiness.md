# December capture and publishing readiness

Draft for local review. Itinerary, participants, equipment, liaison and operational owners remain unconfirmed.

## Minimum capture package

1. A clean Fr. Emmanuel interview: Binzi, Sister Dolores, his formation, present work, what has happened, difficulties, family participation, the Piglet Project model, next objective and useful support.
2. One complete case study with a consenting adult or household: participant account, inputs, actual work and intended/observed result.
3. Operational evidence of what exists now: livestock, pens, feed, agriculture, water, school materials, housing, cooperative work and appropriate procurement records.
4. Community and pastoral context: gatherings, prayer, music, landscapes, roads and ordinary life, with dignity and suitable permission.
5. Horizontal and vertical coverage where practical; prioritize intelligible audio and stable, well-lit footage over optional cinematic shots.

## Phone-friendly field checklist

Before recording: explain intended use; confirm participation/identification/reuse permission; check audio, battery and storage; identify the activity and date. For children, confirm the appropriate guardian and child participation requirements with the local responsible person.

For each sequence: date, suitable location, activity, source/creator, approved identification, permission reference, restrictions and related project. Note factual uncertainties while the people and context are available.

Before leaving: capture the essential activity and interview, confirm key names/figures, save notes with the files, make a second copy where practical, flag strong assets and any material that must remain private. Do not rely on reconstructing metadata later.

## Metadata fields

Asset ID; filename; capture date; location; activity; creator; identification approved for public use; description; interview language; transcription/translation status; private consent reference; allowed website/social use; child/guardian checks if applicable; reuse restrictions; verification status; public caption; publication references.

Blank records may live here; completed participant/consent records must remain in an access-controlled location outside the public repository.

## Intake and archive convention

Naming: `YYYY-MM-DD_BINZI_ACTIVITY_SEQUENCE` with a stable asset ID linking media to notes.

Proposed private folders: incoming originals by date/activity/source; metadata; working edits; approved derivatives; publication records; archive. Preserve originals unchanged. Maintain a separate backup, verify copies can be opened, and keep permission restrictions with every derivative. Select a transfer method and responsible owner before departure.
