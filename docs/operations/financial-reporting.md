# Contribution-to-outcome reporting

Proposed lightweight workflow; confirm actual responsibilities and money flows with Fr. Emmanuel. The accompanying CSV is an empty field schema, not a completed reconciliation or a financial report.

1. Record the original contribution date, channel, amount and currency without converting it silently.
2. Record fees and any conversion separately, including conversion date and source/rate where applicable.
3. Match the bank or project receipt, including the amount actually available locally.
4. Match local purchases/expenditure to their evidence and intended purpose.
5. Connect the expenditure to a dated activity and any observed outcome; retain uncertainty where the evidence stops.
6. Have the designated reviewer check the reconciliation, then approve a redacted public summary separately.

Control questions: Are transfer fees double counted? Are currencies mixed? Are refunds or outgoing transfers being treated as donations? Are dates consistent? Does a visible ledger transfer actually have a confirmed local receipt? Are advance payments distinguished from completed activities?

First worked example: pending real authorized contribution, transfer and purchase records. Do not fabricate a transaction to mark this task complete. Use a stable record identifier across each step; one contribution may fund several purchases, or several contributions may fund one purchase. Do not force an unsupported one-to-one relationship.

Keep completed records and supporting receipts outside the public repository. Public notes should disclose scope, reporting period, summarized amounts, pending reconciliation and the connection to actual evidence without exposing donor or household identities.
