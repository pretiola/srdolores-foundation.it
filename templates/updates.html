Project updates

Our focus is the planned Piglet Project for 60 families in Binzi. These updates trace the work to connect supporters with that mission and prepare for reporting from the community.

December 2026 · Planned visit

Listening to the community in Binzi

An upcoming visit is planned for December. Preparations include an interview with Fr. Emmanuel Kasibante, photographs and stories shared with permission, and an update on local priorities and the Piglet Project. Dates and arrangements are still to be confirmed. Reports from the visit will follow as soon as they are available.

June–August 2026 · Reporting preparations

Preparing to tell the story from Binzi

The project team prepared a field photography and interview plan covering community life, agriculture and pastoral work. It also identified the need to confirm local details and gather further memories and photographs of Sister Dolores. This preparation will help future updates reflect the voices and experiences of the community.

13 July 2026 · Contribution tracking

A public record of EURC contributions

Public ledger tracking was introduced for EURC contributions to the project's Ethereum address. Supporters can follow those transfers using the public ledger linked in our crypto contribution details. Reporting on local spending and results is a separate step.

April 2026 · Staying connected

More ways to follow and contact the foundation

Direct WhatsApp contact and social links were added, photo galleries gained touch controls, and the Daily Celebrations calendar was introduced. These changes made it easier for visitors to stay connected with the foundation's work and spiritual life.

March 2026 · Website renewal

Building a clearer home for the mission

With technical support from Pretiola, the website moved to new hosting, gained a mobile-friendly layout and added photographs supplied by Fr. Emmanuel. Navigation, galleries and bank and digital contribution information were improved to help supporters explore the project.

Historical summaries draw on the foundation's March–May and June–August 2026 progress reports. The December activity follows the September–December planning brief. The 60-family project plan describes intended support; these updates do not establish completed delivery totals.
